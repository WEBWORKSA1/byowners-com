# ByOwners.com: Business Case

## The decision: what ByOwners.com should be

**Build an owner-to-owner marketplace plus education hub, led by real estate.** Homes for sale by owner, rentals by owner, vacation homes and land come first. Vehicles, boats/RVs and small businesses are secondary verticals.

### Why real estate leads

| Option | Search demand | Value per visitor | Lead value | Fit with "ByOwners" | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FSBO homes + rentals (core)** | Very high, evergreen: "for sale by owner", "houses for rent by owner", "sell my house" families | High: real-estate and mortgage ad CPCs are among the highest on the web | High: sellers, buyers needing loans, landlords | Exact | **Lead** |
| Land by owner | Medium | Medium | Medium–high (owner financing, investors) | Exact | Include |
| Vehicles by owner | High volume, crowded | Medium | Low–medium | Good | Secondary |
| Businesses by owner | Low–medium | High | Very high per deal, low volume | Good | Secondary |
| Pure multi-category classifieds | Diffuse | Low | Low | Generic | Avoid |

Before committing ad spend, confirm exact volumes and CPCs in Google Keyword Planner. The ranking above reflects well-established relative demand, not precise counts.

### The bold path (and its catch)

The highest-value lead in this niche is a home seller or buyer who wants an agent. Referral platforms monetize this through **agent referral fees**. Those fees are legally payable only to licensed brokers in most states.

To capture that revenue, partner with or form a licensed referral brokerage. On a $400k sale with a 2.5% side, a 25% referral fee is about $2,500 per closed deal. Closing just 2% of 800 monthly leads that way is roughly **$40k/month**.

The trade-off is that it dilutes the "keep your commission" brand. Run it as an opt-in "need full service?" path. Don't make it the default.

### Compliance guardrails (non-negotiable)

- **RESPA §8:** title, escrow, lender and other settlement-service providers may pay only **flat, fair-market advertising fees**. They may never pay per referral or per closing. The Pro Network copy already says this.
- **TCPA:** phone is optional. The partner-contact consent box is separate, unchecked, and states that "consent is not a condition of purchase".
- **Fair Housing Act:** listings must describe the property, not the people. Discriminatory listings are rejected. The Equal Housing Opportunity statement is on every page.
- **AdSense:**
  - substantial original content (24 long-form guides, 8 tool pages, 60+ glossary terms);
  - required privacy disclosures;
  - no ads near buttons and no "click" prompts;
  - ads.txt and the consent banner are in place.

## Where you should push back on yourself

1. **A static site can't be a self-serve marketplace at scale.** Today, listings are curated by email and published by committing a file. That works to roughly 100 listings a month. Past that, you need the Phase 9 Supabase backend.
2. **AdSense alone won't make this a business.** Even the bold scenario's AdSense is under 20% of revenue. The money is in the Pro Network, upgrades and leads.
3. **Liquidity is the real moat.** An empty marketplace kills trust. Seed supply first:
   - local FSBO outreach (ambassadors);
   - "list free + free pricing worksheet" offers;
   - featuring the first 100 listings free.

   Demand follows inventory.
4. **Trademark exposure is real.** "ByOwner" (singular) is a long-running FSBO brand. Don't stylize the logo like theirs, don't bid on their brand in ads, and keep the independence disclosure visible. The current build follows all three.
