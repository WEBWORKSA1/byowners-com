## Selling land by owner

Land buyers are investors, builders, neighbors and families with a plan. They buy on facts, so the best land listings read like a due-diligence package:

| Item | Why buyers care |
|---|---|
| Survey & legal description | Confirms boundaries and acreage |
| Zoning & permitted uses | Determines what can be built |
| Road access & easements | Legal access is essential for financing and building |
| Utilities (power, water, sewer/septic) | Drives development cost |
| Perc/soil test | Required for septic in many areas |
| Flood zone (FEMA) | Affects insurance and buildable area |

Price land with per-acre comparables of similar size, access and utilities — small parcels usually sell for more per acre than large tracts. Our [land selling guide](/guides/how-to-sell-land-by-owner/) walks through valuation, marketing to builders and investors, and closing.

## Owner financing for land

Banks lend less readily on raw land, which is why many land sales use owner financing: a down payment plus a note and mortgage or deed of trust. It can widen your buyer pool and create monthly income. Read [owner financing explained](/guides/owner-financing-explained/) and use a real estate attorney to document the deal.

## Buying land from an owner

Verify ownership and liens with a title company, confirm access and utilities yourself, check FEMA flood maps, and never pay a deposit to anyone who refuses a title-company escrow.
