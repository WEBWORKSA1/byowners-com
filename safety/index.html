Top red flags

If you see any of these, stop and verify before you pay or share personal information.

Red flagWhy it mattersWhat to do
“Owner” is out of the country and can't show the propertyClassic rental scam — the listing is often copied from a real oneInsist on an in-person or live video tour; verify ownership in county records
Deposit requested by wire, gift card, crypto or payment app before a tourThese payments are nearly impossible to reversePay deposits only after signing a lease or contract, through traceable methods
Rent or price far below comparable propertiesToo-good-to-be-true pricing lures quick decisionsCompare with similar listings; ask why
Last-minute change to closing wiring instructionsBusiness email compromise targets real estate closingsCall your title company or attorney at a number you looked up yourself
Buyer overpays and asks you to refund the differenceThe original check or payment will bounce after you refundNever refund overpayments; accept only verified funds
Pressure to act today and skip inspections or paperworkUrgency prevents verificationSlow down. Legitimate parties accept normal due diligence
