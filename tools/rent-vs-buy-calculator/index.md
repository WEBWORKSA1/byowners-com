## How the comparison works

**Net cost of buying** = down payment + buying closing costs + mortgage payments + property tax + maintenance + insurance/HOA + the return your upfront cash would have earned − your equity after selling costs.

**Net cost of renting** = total rent paid, rising each year by your rent-increase rate.

The **break-even year** is the first year buying's net cost drops below renting's.

## Tips for honest inputs

- Use a conservative appreciation rate. Long-run home price growth has averaged low single digits nationally, with big local swings.
- Maintenance of about 1% of value per year is a common planning figure; older homes often run higher.
- Selling by owner can cut your future selling costs — model a lower figure and see how the break-even moves. Our [commission savings calculator](/tools/commission-savings-calculator/) shows the dollars.
