## Seller vs. buyer closing costs

| Seller usually covers | Buyer usually covers |
|---|---|
| Mortgage payoff and lien releases | Lender origination fees and points |
| Commissions (if any) | Appraisal and credit report |
| Transfer taxes (varies by state) | Lender's title insurance policy |
| Owner's title policy (in many areas) | Prepaid interest, taxes and insurance |
| Prorated taxes and HOA through closing | Inspection, survey (varies) |

Escrow/settlement, attorney and recording fees are commonly split or follow local custom.

## Lower your costs

1. **Get two or three quotes** for title/escrow or attorney services; base title premiums may be regulated, but settlement fees often aren't.
2. **Negotiate credits** instead of price cuts when it helps both sides' cash position.
3. **Sell by owner** to remove the listing-side commission — see the [commission savings calculator](/tools/commission-savings-calculator/).
