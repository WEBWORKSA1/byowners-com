## The metrics, explained

| Metric | Formula | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Net operating income (NOI) | Rent − vacancy − operating expenses (excludes mortgage) | The property's earning power |
| Cap rate | NOI ÷ price | Return if bought with cash; compare across properties |
| Cash flow | NOI − mortgage payment | What lands in your account monthly |
| Cash-on-cash | Annual cash flow ÷ cash invested | Return on your actual dollars |
| DSCR | NOI ÷ debt service | Safety margin lenders look at |

## Don't skip the "invisible" expenses

New landlords underestimate **vacancy**, **maintenance** and **capital expenditures** (roof, HVAC, water heater). Budgeting a percentage of rent for each smooths out the big hits. Self-managing? Keep management at 0% but value your time honestly.
