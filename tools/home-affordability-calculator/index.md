## How we calculate it

1. Your **maximum housing payment** is the lower of: income × housing ratio, or income × total-debt ratio − your other monthly debts.
2. We subtract monthly taxes, insurance and HOA and solve for the largest loan that fits, adding PMI when your down payment is under 20%.
3. **Max price = loan + your down payment.**

## Example

With $110,000 income, $600 in monthly debts, $40,000 down, a 6.5% rate, 1.1% taxes and $1,800 insurance, the housing ratio allows about $2,567/month and the debt ratio about $2,700, so the housing ratio limits you. That supports a home price around the mid-$300,000s.
