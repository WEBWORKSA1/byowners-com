## What goes into your net proceeds

Your net is the sale price minus everything that must be paid at or before closing:

| Cost | Who typically pays | How it's calculated |
|---|---|---|
| Mortgage payoff & liens | Seller | Payoff statement from your servicer |
| Commissions | Negotiable | Percentage of price, or $0 for the listing side when you sell by owner |
| Title, escrow & attorney | Varies by state custom | Flat fees plus a percentage-based title premium |
| Transfer/excise taxes | Varies by state & county | Percentage of price; some states charge none |
| Prorated taxes & HOA | Seller owes through closing day | Daily rate × days |
| Concessions & repairs | Negotiated | Credits you agree to after inspection |

## A worked example

Sell for $400,000 with a $180,000 payoff, no listing commission, a 2.5% buyer-agent fee ($10,000), 1% title/escrow/attorney ($4,000), 0.4% transfer tax ($1,600), and $8,800 in concessions, repairs, prorations and moving costs. Net proceeds: about **$195,600**.
