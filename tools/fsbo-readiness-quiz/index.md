## What the quiz measures

The questions map to the five things that decide most by-owner sales:

1. **Time** — showings, calls and paperwork take hours each week.
2. **Market** — in fast markets buyers find you; in slow ones exposure matters more.
3. **Pricing knowledge** — the right price is the biggest driver of success. See [how to price a home for sale by owner](/guides/how-to-price-a-home-for-sale-by-owner/).
4. **Negotiation and paperwork** — offers, contingencies and disclosures. See [negotiating offers without an agent](/guides/negotiating-offers-without-an-agent/) and [seller disclosure requirements](/guides/seller-disclosure-requirements/).
5. **Priorities** — how you trade money against convenience.

Whatever your score, the [interactive checklist](/checklist/) keeps every step on track.
